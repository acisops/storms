Storm Memos
============

There is a basic introduction to Space Weather `here <https://cxc.cfa.harvard.edu/twiki/bin/view/AcisOps/SpaceWeatherIntro>`_, on the ACIS Ops twiki.

There is also a discussion of ACIS response to radiation storms in the `On Radiation memo <http://cxc.cfa.harvard.edu/acis/reb/OnRadiation.html>`_.

A number of Chandra orbits have presented challenging radiation situations, making us look
hard at the criteria for recommending an SCS 107 interruption of the science loads. Below
are memos that have been written concerning these events.
